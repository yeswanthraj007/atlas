# Pre-registration: weak-signal ensemble v1

Committed before any code for this experiment exists. Results may not change these rules;
any deviation is reported as a deviation.

## Question
Do several individually weak but directionally consistent signals, combined with shrinkage
and traded with cost-aware partial rebalancing, produce a net-of-cost edge that the
individual signals could not demonstrate?

## Motivation (evidence)
- Individually directionally right but under-powered on the S&P 500: 1-month reversal
  (positive in 100% of years, t 1.98), technology peers (86% of years, t 1.22), customer
  momentum (67%, t 0.77), earnings-release text (2024 t 2.89, faded 2025-26).
- Combining many characteristics with shrinkage predicts robustly out of sample
  (Kozak, Nagel & Santosh 2020; Kelly, Malamud & Zhou 2024).
- Cost-aware construction and partial trading toward an aim portfolio are what make ML
  forecasts implementable (Jensen, Kelly, Malamud & Pedersen; Garleanu & Pedersen 2013).

## Universe and data
S&P 1500 (S&P 500 with index-addition dates; S&P 400/600 current members, survivorship
bias disclosed). Daily bars 2016-01 to 2026-10. Costs: liquidity-tier assumptions
(4 / 10 / 25 bps round trip), with 0 / 10 / 30 bps sensitivity.

## Inputs (all point-in-time; cross-sectional ranks centred to [-0.5, 0.5]; missing = 0)
1. reversal_1m  2. mom_12_1  3. low_vol  4. tech-peer catch-up  5. customer momentum
6. earnings-release text score (model retrained each January on releases whose 21-day
   outcome was known by then; a release's score applies for 63 trading days)
7. gross_profitability  8. earnings_yield  9. book_to_price  (S&P 500 names only)

## Model
Target: cross-sectional rank of the next 21-day return. Ridge regression on the inputs,
retrained every January on an expanding window of monthly samples whose labels were known
by then. Baseline for comparison: equal-weight average of the inputs (no fitting).

## Portfolio
Monthly (21 trading days). Aim portfolio: long the top decile, short the bottom decile by
predicted rank, equal weight. Trading rule: w_t = w_{t-1} + tau * (aim_t - w_{t-1})
(Garleanu & Pedersen). Costs charged on |w_t - w_{t-1}| at each stock's tier cost.
Also reported: long-only top-50 portfolio versus SPY, same trading rule.

## Variants (all reported; PBO computed across them)
ridge alpha in {1, 10, 100} x tau in {0.25, 0.5, 1.0}, plus equal-weight x tau = 12.

## Primary specification (fixed now)
ridge alpha = 10, tau = 0.5.

## Evaluation
Walk-forward: train through Dec of year Y-1, trade year Y, for Y = 2018 ... 2026.
Reported: net long-short monthly returns, annualised Sharpe, t-stat of mean net return,
IC by year, by segment (S&P 500 vs mid/small), 2024-2026 sub-period, PBO, minimum
detectable IC, and long-only excess vs SPY with CAPM alpha t-stat.

## Success criteria (all must hold for the primary specification)
1. Net-of-cost long-short mean monthly return t >= 3.0 over 2018-2026.
2. Net-of-cost long-short mean return > 0 over 2024-2026.
3. PBO across the 12 variants < 0.3.
4. Long-only top-50 net CAPM alpha vs SPY t >= 2.0.
If all hold: register "ensemble_v1" as a direction signal; paper-trade before any capital.
If any fails: log as a failed trial with every number reported.

## Trial accounting
Counted as one new trial family (12 variants) in research/trials.jsonl.
