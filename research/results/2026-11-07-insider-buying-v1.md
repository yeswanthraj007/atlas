# Result: opportunistic insider cluster buying — rejected at development

Pre-registration: research/preregistered/2026-11-07-insider-buying-v1.md. Trial #80.
Data: SEC Form 4 quarterly data sets 2013Q1-2026Q3; 30,839 qualifying purchases in the
S&P 1500; the signal is active on about 9% of stock-days.

Lab gates (S&P 1500):
- Gate 1, look-ahead: pass.
- Gate 2, development 2017-2024: IC +0.0015, t +0.25, same sign in 50% of years. **Fail.**
  S&P 500 segment IC +0.0100 (t 1.03); mid/small IC -0.0011 (t -0.18).
  Decile spread +0.12% gross, -0.14% after estimated costs.
- Gates 3 and 4 not reached; the 2025+ holdout stays unopened.

Secondary report (event study, development years only): the 21-day market-adjusted return
after a new insider cluster begins (day after filing), 5,585 events, is +0.12% (t +0.56 across
months). S&P 500 +0.09%, mid/small +0.10%, starts with two or more insiders +0.18% (t 0.34).
By year: 2017 -0.2%, 2018 +0.4%, 2019 -0.2%, 2020 -2.1%, 2021 +0.5%, 2022 +0.7%, 2023 +0.1%, 2024 +0.4%.

Reading: no usable effect in 2017-2024 with this specification. The literature's large effects
come from 1980s-2000s data and mostly from small, thinly covered firms; after 2012 the
public Form 4 signal looks arbitraged. Variants (different windows, purchase-size thresholds,
adding sales, CMP's stricter routine rule) would be new trials with new pre-registrations;
none is run now because the raw effect is about zero.
